🌐 全體旅伴通用必備文件 🌐 Universal Requirements for ALL Travelers

1. 護照有效期 (Passport) 1. Passport Validity

自入境當天起算，至少需有 6 個月以上效期（香港特區/BNO護照需至少 3 個月以上）。

Must have at least 6 months of remaining validity from the date of arrival (at least 3 months for Hong Kong SAR/BNO passports).

2. 返程機票 (Return Ticket) 2. Return / Onward Ticket

必須已開立第 5 天離境之機票證明，以供航空公司櫃檯與海關抽查。

A confirmed departure air ticket for Day 5 must be presented upon boarding or immigration inspection.

3. 線上入國登記卡 (TWAC) 3. Taiwan Online Arrival Card (TWAC)

全面無紙化！抵達前請於官網完成免費填報，存下確認序號或條碼截圖。

Paper arrival cards are gone. Complete the free online form prior to arrival and screenshot the confirmation.

🇭🇰 香港特區 / BNO 必須列印紙本 Paper Print Required

入台證 (Exit & Entry Permit)：
出發前務必於移民署網站線上申辦「港澳居民網路申辦入台證」。
⚠️ 強制規範：核准後必須使用 A4 白色白紙列印出實體紙本，手機出示電子檔恕不接受。登機及入關均需蓋章！

Exit & Entry Permit Required:
Apply online via the National Immigration Agency before departure.
⚠️ MANDATORY: Once approved, you MUST print a physical copy on A4 white paper. Digital displays on phones are NOT accepted.

🇰🇷 韓國護照 (South Korea) 90天免簽證 90 Days Visa-Free

Visa-Exempt Entry：
持有南韓有效護照者享有 90 天免簽證觀光待遇。無須提前辦理簽證，出發前填妥 TWAC 線上入國登記卡即可順暢通關。

Visa-Exempt Entry:
Eligible for visa-free entry for up to 90 days. No prior visa application required. Simply complete the TWAC Online Arrival Card.

🇵🇭 菲律賓護照 (Philippines) 14天免簽證 14 Days Visa-Free

Visa-Free Policy：
享有 14 天觀光免簽證待遇。需備妥效期內護照、第 5 天離境機票與淡水住宿訂單，提前填寫 TWAC 線上入國登記卡。

Visa-Free Policy:
Eligible for 14-day visa-free entry. Prepare a valid passport, return ticket, and accommodation booking. Complete TWAC online beforehand.

🇮🇩 印尼護照 (Indonesia) 條件式免簽 / 簽證 Conditional Visa-Free

ROC Travel Authorization Certificate (TAC)：
若持有美、加、英、日、韓、澳、紐或申根國之有效簽證或居留證，可上網免費申請 TAC 條件式免簽，並列印紙本。無則需申辦實體簽證。

ROC Travel Authorization Certificate (TAC):
If you hold a valid visa/residence permit from US, UK, Japan, Korea, Australia, etc., apply for a free TAC online and print it. Otherwise, apply for a standard visa.

🚨 台灣海關三大重罰法規警告 🚨 Zero Tolerance Customs Rules in Taiwan

🚫 嚴禁攜帶任何肉製品（尤其是豬肉） 🚫 Strict Ban on Meat (especially Pork) 為防範非洲豬瘟，肉乾、火腿、含肉塊泡麵一律嚴禁入境。初犯現場直接開罰 NT$ 200,000 元（約 6,000 美元），未繳清立即遣返！ To prevent Swine Fever, meat jerky, sausages, and meat noodles are prohibited. Fine is NT$200,000 on the spot. Failure to pay leads to deportation!
🚫 嚴禁攜帶新鮮蔬果與植物 🚫 Fresh Produce & Plants Banned 新鮮水果、生鮮蔬菜、植物種子、帶土植株一律不可攜帶入境，如有攜帶必須於海關紅線櫃檯主動申報銷毀。 Fresh fruits, vegetables, seeds, and plants with soil cannot enter Taiwan. Must declare and dispose of them at the red customs channel.
🚫 全面禁止電子煙與加熱菸 🚫 Vapes & E-Cigarettes Banned 台灣法規全面禁止電子煙產品（包括主機、煙油、煙彈與加熱菸）。託運或隨身攜帶均屬違法，會被沒收並處以重額罰鍰。 Vapes, e-cigarettes, liquids, and heated tobacco are strictly illegal. Do not put them in checked or carry-on bags; they will be confiscated with a heavy fine.

💡 台北旅行實用生活須知 💡 Taipei Practical Survival Guide

🚇
捷運車廂內嚴禁飲食 (No Food or Drinks) Strictly NO Food/Drinks in MRT 進入捷運站黃色警戒線後，嚴禁喝水、嚼口香糖或進食，違者最高罰鍰 NT$ 7,500 元。手扶梯請靠右側站立，左側為趕路通行道。 Once past the yellow line at MRT stations, drinking water, chewing gum, and eating are forbidden (Fine: NT$7,500). Stand on the right on escalators.
🔌
電壓與插座規格 (110V Type A/B) Power Plugs & Voltage (110V Type A/B) 台灣電壓為 110V (60Hz)，插座為平行兩腳扁型（與美、日相同）。香港旅伴需攜帶「英規三腳轉接頭」；韓國與印尼旅伴需攜帶「雙圓腳轉接頭」。 Taiwan uses 110V. Plugs are 2 flat pins (same as US/Japan). HK friends need a 3-pin UK adapter. Korean/Indonesian friends need a round-pin European adapter.
👟
服裝與裝備建議 (Gear & Climate) Attire & Gear Suggestions Day 2 的十分與九份有較多步道與石階，請全員務必穿著包覆防滑的球鞋。北部靠海與山區氣候多變，隨身背包內常備一把輕便折疊傘。 Day 2 has many trails and stairs; comfortable sneakers are mandatory. Northern Taiwan weather is unpredictable, always pack a folding umbrella in your daybag.
